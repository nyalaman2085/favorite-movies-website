Top Gun : Maverick

Rating: 4.5 stars

Synopsis

Decades after his rise as a daring pilot, Pete "Maverick" Mitchell returns as a mentor to a new generation of aviators tasked with an almost impossible mission. Haunted by the past and resistant to change, Maverick must confront both his aging identity and unresolved guilt while training young pilots, including the son of his late friend. The film balances breathtaking aerial sequences with emotional depth, turning a story of speed into one about legacy and letting go.
