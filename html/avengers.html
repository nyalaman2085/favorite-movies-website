Avengers : Endgame

Rating: 4.5 stars

Synopsis

Serving as the grand culmination of over a decade of storytelling, the film follows the surviving Avengers as they attempt to reverse the devastating snap that wiped out half of all life. Through time travel, sacrifice, and emotional reckonings, each character faces their past while contributing to one final battle against Thanos. It's both an epic spectacle and a heartfelt farewell, blending action with closure for beloved heroes.
