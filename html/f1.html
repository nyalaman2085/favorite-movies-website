F1

Rating: 5 stars

Synopsis

A high-octane dive into the world of Formula 1 racing, this film follows a seasoned driver mentoring a rising young talent while battling internal demons and the relentless pressure of elite competition. Set against the backdrop of global circuits, it explores not just speed and adrenaline, but ego, legacy, and the razor-thin margin between glory and disaster. Beneath the roar of engines lies a human story about redemption and the cost of ambition in a sport where one mistake can end everything.
