Batman Begins

Rating: 4.7 stars

Synopsis

This origin story reimagines Bruce Wayne's transformation into Batman with a grounded, psychological lens. After witnessing his parents' murder, Bruce travels the world seeking purpose before returning to Gotham to confront corruption and fear itself. Guided by mentors and tested by enemies like Ra's al Ghul, the film builds a hero not from power, but from discipline and trauma, establishing a dark, realistic foundation for the legend of the Caped Crusader.
