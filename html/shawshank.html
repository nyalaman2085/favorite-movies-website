The Shawshank Redemption

Rating: 4 stars

Synopsis

Set within the confines of a harsh prison, the film tells the story of Andy Dufresne, a man wrongly convicted of murder, and his enduring friendship with fellow inmate Red. Through quiet resilience, intelligence, and hope, Andy slowly transforms his bleak surroundings while holding onto a vision of freedom. More than a prison drama, it's a profound meditation on hope, patience, and the unbreakable human spirit.
